Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

In short
  • No account and no sign-in. We run no server and collect no personal data ourselves.
  • Your progress stays on your device.
  • The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. Players under 16 only get non-personalized ads suitable for children.
  • You can change your privacy choices at any time in the game.

Who we are

Nutty Glide (“the game”) is made by an independent developer based in Belgium (“we”), who is responsible for the processing described here. Contact: contact@aisqli.com.

1. What the game keeps on your device

The game saves on your phone or tablet: your progress (best distance, hazelnuts, squirrels and upgrades), your settings (music, sound, vibrations, language), whether you bought “No ads”, and the year of birth entered at the first launch.

The year of birth is only used on your device, to decide whether the game shows ads for children. It is never sent to us or to advertisers. All of this is deleted when you delete the game.

2. Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC). AdMob may let other ad networks it works with, such as Unity Ads (Unity Technologies), provide some of the ads. Ads appear when you choose to watch one for a bonus, and sometimes when you leave the end screen.

  • Players under 16: every ad request is marked as coming from a child under the age of consent. Ads are then non-personalized, limited to content suitable for all ages, and provided only by ad networks that Google certifies for families. The game never asks a child for permission to track.
  • Players 16 and over: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks whether you agree to personalized ads. On iPhone and iPad, Apple asks whether the game may track you; if you refuse, your device's advertising identifier is not used. Without your consent, ads are non-personalized.

Even non-personalized ads use some technical information, such as your IP address, device type, operating system and language, to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud. The ad networks process this information under their own policies:

  • Google: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Unity: Unity game player and app user privacy policy

The legal basis for personalized ads is your consent. For non-personalized ads, measurement and fraud prevention, it is our legitimate interest in keeping the game free.

Changing your choices: in the game, open Settings (the gear on the title screen) and tap “Privacy” to see Google's consent form again. On iPhone and iPad: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android: Settings > Google > Ads, where you can delete your advertising ID.

3. Purchases

The “No ads” purchase is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details; only the fact that the purchase was made is kept on your device. A parental check (a small multiplication) is shown before any purchase.

4. Technical data from the game engine

The game is made with Unity. Unity may collect anonymous technical information, such as the device model, operating system, performance and crash reports, to keep its engine working. See the Unity privacy policy.

5. Children

The game is suitable for everyone, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: players under 16 get the children's mode described above, and a parental check is shown before any purchase and before the age entered can be changed. If you think a child's data was collected by mistake, write to us and we will help you have it deleted.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. As we keep no personal data on our side, most requests concern the ad networks: use the links above, or write to us and we will help. You can also file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

7. This website

This website sets no cookie and uses no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may record visitors' IP addresses for security reasons.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its date.
