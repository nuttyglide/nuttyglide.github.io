Support

A question, a bug, an idea? Write to aiconsulting.tech@proton.me. Tell us your device (for example “iPhone 15” or “Samsung Galaxy A54”) and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.

How do I play?

Tap to jump: the longer you hold, the higher the squirrel jumps. Tap again in mid-air for a double jump. Keep holding while falling to glide. Jump over the brambles, traps and logs, glide over the water, and don't let the fox catch you!

I bought “No ads” but I still see ads

“No ads” removes the ads shown when you leave the end screen. The blue “WATCH AD” buttons stay: they are bonuses you choose (revive, double hazelnuts, free hazelnuts).

My purchase disappeared after reinstalling the game

It usually comes back by itself when the game starts. If not, tap the “No ads” button at the top left of the title screen, then “Restore my purchases”, with the same Apple or Google account you used to buy it.

How do I change the age I entered?

Open Settings (the gear on the title screen), then “Change my age”. A small multiplication is asked first, so that only a grown-up can change it.

How do I change my ad choices?

Open Settings (the gear on the title screen), then “Privacy”. See the privacy policy for details.

My progress is gone

Your progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the game, or moving to another phone, starts the game over.
