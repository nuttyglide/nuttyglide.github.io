How to play

One finger. An endless forest.

Learn it in a second, and you won't want to stop.

  1. Tap to jump

    Tap again in mid-air for a double jump, or bounce off a wall.

  2. Hold to jump higher

    The longer you hold, the higher the flying squirrel jumps.

  3. Keep holding to glide

    Spread your wings and glide over the water, the brambles and the traps.

FAQ

Questions and answers

Is Nutty Glide free?

Yes, the game is free to download and play. An optional “No ads” purchase removes the ads shown between runs.

On which devices can I play?

On iPhone and iPad (iOS and iPadOS 15 or later) from the App Store, and on Android phones and tablets from Google Play.

Can I play without internet?

Yes! The game works offline, perfect for car trips and flights. You only need a connection for the world leaderboard and for ads.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes, it is rated 4+. Players under 16 only get non-personalized ads suitable for children, and a parental check is shown before any purchase.

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only: there is no account to create. Deleting the game, or moving to another phone, starts the game over.

I have a question or found a bug

Have a look at the support page or write to contact@aisqli.com.
